    References¶

    The literature the physics and validation pages rely on, grouped by area. Each page also lists its own sources next to the equations that use them. The papers are not redistributed with axionMultiPhysics; the arXiv numbers below lead to them.

    Lumped-element detectors and the optimal scan¶

    • S. Chaudhuri, K. D. Irwin, P. W. Graham, J. Mardon, arXiv:1803.01627 (optimal impedance matching and scan strategy); S. Chaudhuri et al., arXiv:1904.05806 (Bode–Fano limit). Used in scan_and_signal.md and readout_circuit.md.
    • J. W. Foster, N. L. Rodd, B. R. Safdi, arXiv:1711.10489 (axion field statistics, likelihood and look-elsewhere effect). Used in scan_and_signal.md.
    • J. N. Benabou, J. W. Foster, Y. Kahn, B. R. Safdi, C. P. Salemi, arXiv:2211.00008, Phys. Rev. D 108, 035009 (2023) (lumped-element detectors beyond the magnetoquasistatic limit). Compared in fullwave.md (the paper's dipole radiation Q) and comsol_import.md (the paper's COMSOL models, which are not distributed); methods in multipole_scan.md.
    • A. AlShirawi et al., arXiv:2302.14084, Phys. Rev. D 112, 052001 (2025) (DMRadio pickup and effective-inductance conventions). Used in multipole_scan.md.
    • Y. Kahn, B. R. Safdi, J. Thaler, arXiv:1602.01086 (ABRACADABRA); J. L. Ouellet et al., arXiv:1810.12257 and arXiv:1901.10652 (ABRACADABRA-10 cm results and instrument).
    • DMRadio-m³, arXiv:2204.13781, Phys. Rev. D 106, 103008 (2022); DMRadio-GUT, arXiv:2203.11246, Phys. Rev. D 106, 112003 (2022); DMRadio-Core, V. Ankel et al., arXiv:2604.16602, Phys. Rev. D 114, 092004 (2026); DMRadio-50L, arXiv:2210.07215 (SciPost Phys. Proc. 12, 036 (2023)) and arXiv:2607.05771. Compared, quantity by quantity, in published_benchmarks.md.
    • Axion–photon coupling of the QCD axion: G. Grilli di Cortona et al., arXiv:1511.02867; lattice values of C_γ, arXiv:2603.29153 (the default, C_γ = 1.77) and arXiv:2609.22698. Used in scan_and_signal.md.

    Readout and dc SQUIDs¶

    • V. Ankel et al., "Noise limits for dc SQUID readout of high-Q resonators below 300 MHz", J. Appl. Phys. 138, 094505 (2025), arXiv:2504.20398. The basis of squid_chain.md.
    • A. A. Clerk et al., Rev. Mod. Phys. 82, 1155 (2010) (quantum noise and measurement).

    Cavity haloscopes¶

    • P. Sikivie, Phys. Rev. Lett. 51, 1415 (1983); Phys. Rev. D 32, 2988 (1985) (the haloscope).
    • S. Al Kenany et al., arXiv:1611.07123 (HAYSTAC design); B. M. Brubaker et al., arXiv:1706.08388 (HAYSTAC analysis); HAYSTAC Collaboration, arXiv:2301.09721 (Phase II); M. Malnou et al., arXiv:1809.06470 (squeezed-state receiver); D. Kim et al., arXiv:2001.05605 (scan-rate conventions). Used in haloscope_sensitivity.md.
    • B. M. Brubaker, PhD thesis, arXiv:1801.00835 (definitions of Q_L, β); P. Balleyguier, Part. Accel. 57, 113 (1997) (external Q of a probed pillbox). Used in coupling.md.
    • Published haloscope runs whose parameters axionmultiphysics.haloscope_presets uses (the module gives the full references): arXiv:1804.05750, arXiv:2504.07279, arXiv:1610.02580, arXiv:2008.01853, arXiv:2210.10961, arXiv:2205.05574, arXiv:2407.18586, arXiv:2012.09498, arXiv:1903.06547.
    • Published cavity simulations compared in cavity_reproductions.md, cavity_tracking.md and cavity_xsec.md: RADES, arXiv:1803.01243 and arXiv:2002.07639; QUAX, arXiv:2004.02754, arXiv:2201.04223 and arXiv:2410.07774; CAPP multiple-cell cavities, arXiv:1710.06969 and arXiv:2205.01319; HAYSTAC one-rod cavity, arXiv:2006.01248; TASEH, arXiv:2205.01477; BI-RME, arXiv:2107.03137; Stern et al. (multi-post cavities), arXiv:1603.06990; KLASH, arXiv:1707.06010; ADMX empty-cylinder modes (PhD thesis), arXiv:2408.03444.

    Surface resistance¶

    • E. Métral, arXiv:1108.1643; D. Valentinis, arXiv:2003.06619 (anomalous skin effect); G. Ciovati, arXiv:1501.07398 (RF superconductivity); D. Gonnella et al., arXiv:1509.04127 (trapped-flux losses). Used in surface_resistance.md and the SRF external benchmarks.

    SRF cavities and heterodyne detection¶

    • B. Aune et al., arXiv:physics/0003011 (TESLA cavities); N. Juntong, R. M. Jones, arXiv:0910.1305 (low-loss cells); R. Lasenby, arXiv:1912.11056, and A. Berlin et al., arXiv:1912.11048 (heterodyne axion detection); S. Holmes et al., arXiv:1306.5022 (Project X reference design, the cell geometry). Used in srf/README.md and sensitivity.md.

    Superconductor critical surfaces¶

    • L. Bottura, IEEE Trans. Appl. Supercond. 10, 1054 (2000); A. Godeke et al., Supercond. Sci. Technol. 19, R100 (2006); L. Bottura, B. Bordini, IEEE Trans. Appl. Supercond. 19, 1521 (2009); F. Grilli et al., "Self-consistent modeling of the Ic of HTS devices: how accurate do models really need to be?", IEEE Trans. Appl. Supercond. 24, 8000508 (2014). Used in hts.md.

    Numerical methods¶

    • MFEM: R. Anderson et al., Comput. Math. Appl. 81, 42 (2021); hypre (BoomerAMG, AMS); METIS; Eigen; STRUMPACK.
    • Y. Nakatsukasa, O. Sète, L. N. Trefethen, SIAM J. Sci. Comput. 40, A1494 (2018) (the AAA rational approximation behind adaptive_sweep.md).
