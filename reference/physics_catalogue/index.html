    Physics catalogue (beta)¶

    The physics catalogue is the registry that model documents (designs with a physics section) are validated against (axmp validate); this version does not solve them: the physics types, the coupling terms between them, and the route table, one row per combination of physics, couplings, geometry class and study type that an engine of this version solves, with what the row offers and what it needs. It is compiled into every build and is the same in each, so a document validates the same way everywhere. Everything on this page is beta, and no route is implemented in this version: a model document is validated and resolved, and a planar or axisymmetric one is meshed (axmp mesh), but none is solved.

    Print it with axmp physics (--routes adds the route table, --type T restricts it to one type, --all prints the details, --json the machine form of schema axionmultiphysics.physics_catalogue/1), or in Python with axionmultiphysics.physics(routes=True, type=None).

    Physics types¶

    type status title publishes
    fluid.incompressible beta Incompressible flow (Stokes and Navier-Stokes) u, p
    heat.transfer beta Heat transfer in solids and fluids T
    electrochem.current_distribution beta Primary and secondary current distribution phi
    chem.reactor beta Reaction engineering (0D reactors) c_<species>
    electrochem.porous_electrode beta Porous electrode (1D) none
    current.conduction absent Electric currents (conduction) V
    solid.linear absent Linear elastic solid displacement
    species.transport absent Transport of diluted species none

    Data-key types: number, boolean, names (a list of names), boundary (a boundary name), number_vector (a list of numbers), vector (a field expression per component: (x, y) planar, (r, z, theta) axisymmetric, (x, y, z) in 3D) and meridional_vector ((x, y), (r, z) and (x, y, z)). Option values are listed as text; an integer option is written as a number in a document.

    fluid.incompressible (beta)¶

    Incompressible flow (Stokes and Navier-Stokes). Aliases (names other codes use; a document writes the name above): laminar_flow, creeping_flow. Publishes the fields u, p.

    option type values default highest geometry order
    equation enum stokes, navier_stokes none (to be written) any
    viscous_form enum stress, laplacian stress any
    convection enum standard, skew standard any
    order integer 2 2 any
    condition kind data keys aliases
    wall velocity_m_s (vector; m/s; field expression; optional) no_slip
    inlet velocity_m_s (vector; m/s; field expression) none
    outlet pressure_Pa (number; Pa; optional) none
    open none open_boundary
    traction traction_Pa (vector; Pa; field expression) boundary_stress
    slip none none
    symmetry none none
    periodic partner (boundary); translation_m (number_vector; m); pressure_drop_Pa (number; Pa; optional); flow_rate (number; m^2/s planar (per metre of depth), m^3/s in 3D; optional) periodic_flow_condition
    source kind data keys aliases
    body_force force_N_m3 (vector; N/m^3; field expression) volume_force
    geometry quantities
    planar u [m/s] (velocity (u_x, u_y)); u_x [m/s]; u_y [m/s]; u_mag [m/s]; p [Pa]; vorticity [1/s] (du_y/dx - du_x/dy); wall_shear [Pa] (traction by the fluid on the wall along t = n rotated +90 deg); wall_shear_mag [Pa]; volume [m^2/s] (flux only: per metre of depth, positive out of the domain); mass [kg/(m s)] (flux only: per metre of depth, positive out of the domain)
    axisymmetric u [m/s] (velocity (u_r, u_z, u_theta)); u_r [m/s]; u_z [m/s]; u_theta [m/s]; u_mag [m/s]; p [Pa]; vorticity [1/s] (azimuthal component du_r/dz - du_z/dr); wall_shear [Pa] (traction by the fluid on the wall along t = n rotated +90 deg); wall_shear_mag [Pa] (includes the swirl part); wall_shear_theta [Pa] (the swirl part of the wall traction); volume [m^3/s] (flux only: 3D total, positive out of the domain); mass [kg/s] (flux only: 3D total, positive out of the domain)
    3d u [m/s] (velocity (u_x, u_y, u_z)); u_x [m/s]; u_y [m/s]; u_z [m/s]; u_mag [m/s]; p [Pa]; vorticity [1/s] (magnitude of curl u); wall_shear_mag [Pa] (magnitude only (no tangent frame in 3D)); volume [m^3/s] (flux only: positive out of the domain); mass [kg/s] (flux only: positive out of the domain)

    Not implemented:

    • solving through the model document: every route of this type is not implemented in this version
    • periodic conditions through the document (they need a periodic instance mesh; roadmap item 'periodic flow')
    • slip and symmetry conditions with Navier-Stokes, and in axisymmetric geometry
    • the swirl-free axisymmetric formulation: an axisymmetric flow has the three components (u_r, u_z, u_theta)
    • inflow by flow rate, backflow stabilization and the directional do-nothing outlet (roadmap item F2b)
    • inductionless MHD, the equal-order (GLS) discretization and turbulence models as options
    • a force on a moving wall with the Laplacian viscous form on the Navier-Stokes rows

    Heat transfer in solids and fluids. Aliases (names other codes use; a document writes the name above): heat_transfer_in_solids, heat_transfer_in_fluids. Publishes the fields T.

    condition kind data keys aliases

    heat_flux q_W_m2 (number; W/m^2) none
    total_flux q_W_m2 (number; W/m^2) none
    convection h_W_m2K (number; W/(m^2 K)); T_ext_K (number; K) convective_heat_flux
    insulation none thermal_insulation
    outflow none none
    source kind data keys aliases
    heat_source q_W_m3 (number; W/m^3; field expression; optional); P_W (number; W; optional; P_W_m in a planar model) none
    prescribed_advection velocity_m_s (meridional_vector; m/s; field expression) velocity_field
    geometry quantities
    planar T [K]; heat_flux_n [W/m^2] (conductive flux -k grad T . n, positive out of the domain); heat [W/m] (flux only: conductive plus advected, per metre of depth, positive out of the domain); heat_conductive [W/m] (flux only: conductive part, per metre of depth, positive out of the domain)
    axisymmetric T [K]; heat_flux_n [W/m^2] (conductive flux -k grad T . n, positive out of the domain); heat [W] (flux only: conductive plus advected, 3D total, positive out of the domain); heat_conductive [W] (flux only: conductive part, 3D total, positive out of the domain)
    3d T [K]; heat_flux_n [W/m^2] (conductive flux -k grad T . n, positive out of the domain); heat [W] (flux only: conductive plus advected, 3D total, positive out of the domain); heat_conductive [W] (flux only: conductive part, 3D total, positive out of the domain)

    Not implemented:

    • solving through the model document: every route of this type is not implemented in this version
    • a transient heat solve
    • heat transfer in 3D
    • a conductivity or a heat source that differs from region to region (one expression over the instance until the transport operator takes data per region)
    • a heat capacity rho cp that differs between the regions of an advected instance (conjugate heat transfer)
    • SUPG on curved (isoparametric) elements
    • quadrilateral elements
    • the heat-to-heat interface term and thermal contact

    stokes_planar¶

    • Status: not implemented yet.
    • Physics: fluid.incompressible; couplings: none; geometry: planar; study: stationary.
    • Selected by: equation = stokes.
    • Engine: StokesSolver.
    • Needs: the mesher, an MPI build, the fluids library, any number of ranks.
    • Condition kinds offered (fluid.incompressible): wall, inlet, outlet, open, traction, slip, symmetry.
    • Not offered (fluid.incompressible): periodic: not_exposed: the engine needs a topologically periodic mesh, and the instance mesh has no boundary element on an interior face; exposing it takes a periodic instance mesh, the partition rule's exception for the partner and matching nodes on the two sides (roadmap item 'periodic flow').
    • Options (fluid.incompressible): equation: stokes (default `);viscous_form:stress,laplacian(defaultstress);order:2(default2`).
    • Material blocks (fluid.incompressible): fluid.
    • Uniform over the instance with viscous_form=laplacian and a force output (fluid.incompressible): fluid.mu_Pa_s (engine_refuses: StokesSolver::physical_reaction).
    • Element types: triangle, quadrilateral.
    • Force output: physical_reaction.
    • Layer rules: geometry_order_exceeds_field_order.
    • a force output with the Laplacian viscous form needs one viscosity over the instance (the engine's physical reaction is defined for a constant viscosity only).
    • periodic conditions are not exposed yet (roadmap item 'periodic flow').
    • any number of MPI ranks for the solve; outputs and point sampling on rank 0 from the saved solution.

    navier_stokes_steady_planar¶

    • Status: not implemented yet.
    • Physics: fluid.incompressible; couplings: none; geometry: planar; study: stationary.
    • Selected by: equation = navier_stokes.
    • Engine: NavierStokesSolver.
    • Needs: the mesher, an MPI build, the fluids library, any number of ranks.
    • Condition kinds offered (fluid.incompressible): wall, inlet, outlet, open, traction.
    • Not offered (fluid.incompressible): periodic: engine_refuses.
    • Not offered (fluid.incompressible): slip: engine_refuses.
    • Not offered (fluid.incompressible): symmetry: engine_refuses.
    • Options (fluid.incompressible): equation: navier_stokes (default `);viscous_form:stress,laplacian(defaultstress);convection:standard,skew(defaultstandard);order:2(default2`).
    • Material blocks (fluid.incompressible): fluid, density.
    • Uniform over the instance (fluid.incompressible): density.
    • Uniform over the instance with viscous_form=laplacian and a force output (fluid.incompressible): fluid.mu_Pa_s (layer_rule: force_with_laplacian_form).
    • Element types: triangle, quadrilateral.
    • Force output: reaction: walls at rest only with the laplacian form.
    • Layer rules: force_with_laplacian_form, geometry_order_exceeds_field_order.
    • Solver settings (fluid.incompressible): newton_rtol (number, default 1e-12), newton_maxit (integer, default 30), n_picard (integer, default 3), line_search (boolean, default true), residual_floor_factor (number, default 4.0).
    • density is one constant over the instance: the engine takes one density per solve.
    • a force output is the engine's reaction of the solved form: the physical force with the stress form; with the Laplacian form the force on walls at rest only.
    • no slip, symmetry or periodic condition with convection.
    • any number of MPI ranks for the solve; outputs and point sampling on rank 0 from the saved solution.

    Layer rules¶

    Rules of this layer that are stricter than the engine on purpose; each route lists the ones it applies.

    rule what it refuses
    insulation_on_throughflow insulation is refused on a boundary that the advecting velocity crosses (heat.insulation_on_throughflow); the engine takes a zero flux anywhere
    outflow_with_conservative_flux outflow is refused with the conservative_flux form, where a zero total flux would block the advected heat (on a row without advection too, so that the name means the same on every heat row); the engine takes it as a zero flux and only the Python binding refuses the name, which this layer mirrors
    force_with_laplacian_form with the Laplacian viscous form a force output is accepted on walls at rest only (output.force_needs_stress_form), and needs one viscosity over the instance
    per_depth_keys an absolute input is written per metre of depth in a planar model (current_A_m, P_W_m) and as the 3D total otherwise (current_A, P_W) (model.per_depth_key)
    geometry_order_exceeds_field_order mesh.geometry_order may not exceed the smallest field order of the instances (model.geometry_order_exceeds_field_order)
    uniform_capacity_in_stagnant_part density and thermal.cp_J_kgK are one constant over an advected heat instance, in its stagnant parts as well, although a steady solve needs the capacity only where the velocity is not zero
